# Document-Structure Investigation: Chemistry Question Bank and Answer Companion

**Inspection date:** 2026-10-30  
**Scope:** read-only comparison of `Revision12.pdf` with `chemistry_answer_companion/answer_companion.pdf`; no PDF was rebuilt or altered for this investigation.

## Executive conclusion

The current 40-page `answer_companion.pdf` is an **answer key with question numbers, difficulty tags, and original-source page references**. It is **not** an integrated Question + Answer document and is not a meaningful partial mixture: every question number appears as a locator, but none of the 500 complete source stems appears in its corresponding answer block. All 500 entries have a non-empty answer. The question wording is deliberately left in the separate inventory; the cover says it is “kept in the separate inventory to avoid duplicating the source bank.”

The original `Revision12.pdf` contains 500 sequential, text-searchable, open-response questions in seven units over 28 Letter pages. The source questions and the current answer blocks can be joined unambiguously by question number/ID; all source-page and `[M]`/`[H]` references align. A separate integrated edition is technically straightforward to produce from the searchable source text and existing answer database, with no OCR and no source graphics to extract. It should be generated as a **new, separate PDF**, leaving the existing answer key untouched.

## Evidence and inspection method

- Extracted text from every page of both PDFs; enumerated every source question marker and every answer-entry heading.
- Re-ran the repository’s read-only `extract_questions()` parser and compared all 500 extracted source records with `question_inventory.json`: question text, number, source page, and difficulty tag match **500/500**.
- Delimited all 500 answer blocks in the companion by their printed question number; checked the answer label/content, complete-stem presence, and source-page/tag reference for each corresponding question.
- Rendered and visually compared representative pages only. The rendered comparison is evidence of overall structure and typography, not a visual review of every source page or every answer entry.
- This is a **document-structure** investigation, not a fresh subject-matter audit of all 500 answers. Use the existing academic audit and verification reports for answer-review scope and qualifications.

## Risks, rights, and limits

- **Copyright/provenance:** this report includes the original full question text for per-item comparison. The repository does not establish redistribution rights for the source question bank, fonts, or other assets. Do not treat possession of `Revision12.pdf` or this investigation as permission to publish copied question text; confirm applicable rights before distributing an integrated edition. No proprietary images were identified or reused.
- **Text fidelity:** superscripts/subscripts and chemical notation are text spans, sometimes with mixed fonts. The parser and inventory agree exactly, but an integrated layout still needs targeted visual proofreading for glyphs, spacing, and line wraps.
- **Answer quality:** the structural test confirms non-empty answer blocks and correct question references; it does not assert that every answer is chemically correct or that every answer block is character-for-character identical under all PDF text-extraction normalizations. The final release and academic audit reports document the existing review scope and remaining qualifications.
- **Visual sampling:** representative original/companion pages were rendered and compared. The source’s full question text and companion entries were compared computationally for all 500, but not every source page was visually inspected in this investigation.
- **Pagination:** long solutions and unit boundaries may produce sparse pages or force a pair to continue. A pilot covering short, equation-heavy, numerical, and long entries is needed before fixing the design/page-count estimate.
